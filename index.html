About

Alex Tait is an illustrator and motion designer based between Bath and London. His practice spans commercial character work and a personal series of dark gradient creature illustrations — bold, warm, and occasionally odd.

His illustration and animation has been commissioned by Apple, Google, Spotify and Adidas, spanning broadcast, social, packaging and print. Represented by Jelly for commissions worldwide.
