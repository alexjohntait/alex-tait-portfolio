IdeaFarm

ClientIdeaFarm
CategoryAdvertising
Year2020

A cast of characters created for IdeaFarm, a production agency working across multiple sectors. The brief was essentially a visual taxonomy: a set of figures that could represent the different industries the brand operates in, used across their site and materials. Each character was built to feel distinct but part of the same world, with Alex's expressive line work giving the roster personality rather than just function.
